Try it

Drag across this sentence, then switch presets.

Every day, hundreds of millions of people highlight text. They do it to copy a quote, to keep their place, to think. And every time, the browser answers with the same flat rectangle it has drawn since 1995. HyperSelect replaces that rectangle with something alive.

Select any text on this page to see the current preset, then pick another from the bar at the bottom or use the arrow keys. Each preset is a small GLSL shader, many with particles, drawn behind the text while the selection itself stays the browser's own.

One feature, done properly

HyperSelect does one thing. There is no settings panel and no account. The entire product is the moment between your cursor landing on a word and something beautiful happening underneath it, so all of the attention went there.

Effects run on the GPU in a transparent WebGL layer behind your content. Each selected line gets a shader-drawn surface and a population of instanced sprites. The layer lives in document space, so when you scroll, the lightning scrolls with the paragraph it belongs to.
