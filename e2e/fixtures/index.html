Every day, hundreds of millions of people highlight text. They do it to copy a quote, to keep their place, to think. And every time, the browser answers with the same flat rectangle it has drawn since 1995.

Selections cross emphasis, strong type, larger words, inline code and links without losing their shape.

This paragraph lives in its own scrolling box. Only the part of it that the box shows should ever be highlighted, however far the box has been scrolled and however much of the text is selected. The rest of it stays hidden, exactly as the text does, and the effect never spills over the content around the box.

Scaled content keeps the effect glued to every glyph, because the geometry comes straight from the browser.

Rotated text keeps the native highlight, since a flat effect could never follow its slant.
